Modern Clinical Infrastructure

State-of-the-Art Diagnostic Facilities

Equipped with hospital-grade, point-of-care technology to deliver rapid answers and same-day therapeutic adjustments.

CGM Sensor Clinic

Real-time

Seamless placement, calibration, and cloud telemetry analysis for Dexcom G7, Abbott FreeStyle Libre 3, and Medtronic Guardian sensors.

  • Zero fingerstick continuous tracking
  • Automated alerts for hyper/hypoglycemia

Thyroid Ultrasound & FNA

Same-Day

High-frequency color Doppler imaging for accurate thyroid nodule stratification (TI-RADS) with minimally invasive in-office needle biopsy.

  • Painless ultrasound-guided precision
  • Molecular testing on thyroid nodule cells

Point-of-Care Rapid Lab

15-Min Results

CLIA-certified on-site lab delivering fast HbA1c, microalbuminuria, lipid panels, and rapid fingerstick blood ketone measurements.

  • Discuss test results during the appointment
  • Immediate prescription dose adjustments

Bone Density & Metabolic DEXA

Advanced

Gold-standard dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry for early osteoporosis detection, fracture risk calculation, and visceral fat tracking.

  • Precise T-score & Z-score diagnostics
  • Skeletal muscle & lean mass quantification

Diabetic Foot Care Suite

Preventative

Early peripheral neuropathy assessment utilizing calibrated Semmes-Weinstein monofilaments and Doppler arterial circulation checks.

  • Preventative ulcer & neuropathy screening
  • Custom therapeutic diabetic footwear guidance

Insulin Pump & Infusion Suite

Dedicated

Comfortable private treatment bays for intravenous osteoporosis therapy, biologics, and hands-on smart insulin pump initiation.

  • Omnipod, Tandem & Medtronic training
  • Relaxing environment with registered nurses
