Patient Questions

Common Inquiries

Find quick answers to common questions about preparing for your visit, accepted insurances, and accessing lab results.

View All FAQs

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

While we accept direct bookings, many HMO insurance plans require a referral from your primary care physician to cover specialist visits. Please check your specific policy.

Do I need to fast before my first visit?

If you are having lab work drawn (such as fasting glucose or lipid panels), we recommend fasting for 8-12 hours prior. Drink plenty of water.

How do I log my blood sugar in the dashboard?

Once logged in to the Patient Dashboard, navigate to the "Health Metrics" tab. You can manually enter readings or sync data from compatible continuous glucose monitors (CGMs).
