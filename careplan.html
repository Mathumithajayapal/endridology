What Every Care Plan Guarantees

Regardless of which care package you enroll in, you receive our uncompromising standard of patient-centered clinical excellence.

Fellowship-Trained Endocrinologists

Your care is directly managed by board-certified specialist physicians—never outsourced to distant algorithmic call centers or mid-level triage.

Rapid Point-of-Care Testing

CLIA-certified in-office capillary fingerstick testing delivers lab-quality HbA1c results in 10 minutes so medications are adjusted face-to-face.

Continuous Remote Telemetry

Automatic cloud sync with Dexcom Clarity, LibreView, and Glooko. We observe glucose patterns between visits to prevent subtle complications.

Registered Dietitian Coaching

Work directly with licensed CDCES dietitians for carbohydrate macro-partitioning, glycemic index education, and sustainable metabolic weight loss.

Prior Authorization Advocacy

Our navigators handle complex insurance paperwork, formulary step-therapy appeals, and enroll you in manufacturer $25 copay assistance programs.

24/7 Portal & Emergency Triage

Access your test results instantly online and contact our on-call clinical nurse team anytime you experience severe glycemic variances or pump errors.

Your Care Plan Journey

What to expect from Day 1 to ongoing metabolic stability. We guide every step with compassionate, clinical precision.

01 Days 1 - 7

Deep Clinical Workup

60-minute in-person consultation, comprehensive baseline laboratory panel (HbA1c, full thyroid axis, lipids), and initial device calibration.

Milestone: Individualized Care Plan Blueprint established.
02 Days 8 - 21

Telemetry Calibration
