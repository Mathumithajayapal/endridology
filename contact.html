What insurance plans do you accept?

We are in-network with most major PPO plans, including BlueCross, Aetna, and Cigna. Please contact billing to verify HMO referrals.

How long does a thyroid panel take?

Blood draws take about 10 minutes in the clinic. Comprehensive results are typically uploaded to your Patient Portal within 3-5 business days.
