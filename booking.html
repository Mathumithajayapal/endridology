How to Prepare for Your Visit

A few simple preparatory steps help our clinicians perform accurate diagnostics and formulate your personalized metabolic plan without delays.

Fasting Instructions

If having fasting glucose, C-peptide, or lipid panels drawn, fast for 8 to 12 hours prior. Drink plenty of water to stay hydrated.

Water is allowed & recommended

Current Medications

Bring your original medication bottles or an updated list, including thyroid replacement doses, insulin units, and over-the-counter supplements.

Exact dosages matter

Sync CGM & Pump Data

If using Dexcom, FreeStyle Libre, Omnipod, or Tandem, sync your device to the cloud 24 hours prior or bring your handheld reader.

Enables 90-day AGP analysis

Prior Scans & Reports

Bring any recent thyroid ultrasound images, fine-needle biopsy pathology reports, or bone density DEXA scans from the past 2 years.

Uploadable via Patient Portal

Booking & Visit FAQs

Answers to common inquiries regarding scheduling, referrals, cancellations, and visit prep.

How fast can I be seen by a doctor?

For urgent conditions such as newly diagnosed Type 1 diabetes, rapid thyroid swelling, or gestational diabetes, we offer expedited appointments within 24 to 48 hours. Routine consults are typically scheduled within 5 to 7 business days.

Do I need a primary care physician referral?

Our clinic accepts self-referrals and direct bookings. However, if your insurance policy is an HMO or specialized managed care plan, your insurer may require a written referral to approve coverage. We recommend checking with your insurer.

What is your cancellation & rescheduling policy?

We kindly request at least 24 hours' advance notice if you need to cancel or change your time. You can easily reschedule online via the Patient Portal or by calling our front desk so we may offer the slot to a waiting patient.

Can I receive my lab results during the visit?

Yes! Our CLIA-certified on-site lab performs rapid point-of-care fingerstick HbA1c, microalbumin, lipid panels, and blood ketone tests with complete results ready in under 15 minutes, allowing immediate prescription titration.
